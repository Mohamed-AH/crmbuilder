Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026 · applies to the free beta at this address

The short version. Your CRM lives on your own device. It is only copied to our server if you sign in, and only so you can use it on more than one device. We do not sell anything, we do not run advertising or third-party analytics, and you can take everything with you or delete it at any time from Settings.

What we hold, and why

If you never sign in: nothing. The app runs entirely in your browser and stores your modules and records there. No account exists and we have no way to identify you.

If you sign in with Google, we receive and store your email address, your name, and your profile picture URL. We use these to identify your workspace, to show who you are in the app, and to name you to colleagues if you join a team. We do not receive your Google password and we ask for no access to any other Google service.

Your CRM contents — the modules, fields and records you create — are stored on our server so they can sync between your devices and, if you are on a team, be shared with that team.

Operational records: we keep a log of sign-ins and syncs, used to see whether the service is working and how much of it is being used. These expire automatically after 90 days.

Problem reports: if you use Report a problem, we store what you wrote plus your app version, the screen you were on, your browser, your sync status, how many modules and records you have, and up to ten recent error messages from your browser's console. The app tells you this before you send. Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Who else sees it

The service runs on Render (hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (database). Both hold the data on our behalf in order to run the service. Sign-in is provided by Google.

If a problem report is sent, we may receive a notification containing your email address and what you wrote — the diagnostic details and any error messages stay in our database and are not forwarded anywhere.

That is the complete list. There is no advertising network, no analytics product, and nobody buys anything from us.

Teams

A workspace belongs to an organisation, not to a person. Everyone on a team can see and edit everything in that team's workspace, and records show who added them. If you bring your own records into a team when you join, the app says so first and asks. Leaving a team, or being removed from one, gives you a fresh empty workspace and removes the team's data from your device the next time it connects.

Getting your data out, and deleting it

  • Export: Settings → Export backup gives you everything as a JSON file, and each module exports as CSV. No approval needed and no waiting.
  • Delete: Settings → Danger zone deletes the workspace. Deleting the last account in an organisation deletes that organisation's workspace too.
  • Ask us: if you want your account removed and cannot do it yourself, contact us at the address below.

Where it is kept, and how long

Data sits on Render and MongoDB Atlas infrastructure, currently in their US regions. We keep your workspace for as long as your account exists. Deleted records leave a marker for up to 180 days so the deletion can reach your other devices; the contents are removed immediately. Activity logs and problem reports expire after 90 days.

This is a free beta, and backups are limited. The database tier we are on provides no point-in-time recovery. We take a daily export, so the worst case is losing up to a day. Please keep your own JSON backup of anything you would be upset to lose.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Sessions are httpOnly cookies. We never see or store a password. Each account's data on a shared computer is kept in a separate local store, so one person's work cannot be read or uploaded by the next person to sign in.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing the date.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data: use Report a problem in the app, or contact the person who invited you to the beta.
