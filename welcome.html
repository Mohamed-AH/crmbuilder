What it is

Shape it around your work

Start from a prebuilt module — contacts, deals, invoices, renewals — or build one field by field. Rename anything later and the data comes with it.

Everyone on a team sees every module. Roles govern what people can do, not what they can see.

Nothing slips quietly

Filter any module by what is due or overdue, get one digest a morning in your team chat, and draft the chaser for an overdue invoice in a single press.

The digest says how many, never which — a chat channel usually has more people in it than your CRM does.

It stays yours

It works with the connection off and syncs when it comes back, record by record, so two people editing different things both keep their edit. Export the whole workspace as CSV or JSON whenever you want.

A delete cannot be undone, and on a team it deletes for everybody.
