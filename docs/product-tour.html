The problem isn't discipline

A five-person business signs up for a big-name CRM, uses three features, pays for forty, and is back in a spreadsheet within two months. That isn't a failure of follow-through.

What usually happens

The software arrives with its own idea of how business works — Accounts, Opportunities, Leads — and the job becomes translating your world into its vocabulary.

A plumber doesn't have a sales funnel. They have jobs, quotes and callbacks. Fields that don't apply get left blank, blank fields make the data untrustworthy, and untrustworthy data gets abandoned.

What CRM Builder does

It asks a question no other CRM asks first: what do you want to track? Then it builds around that answer.

Jobs, quotes and callbacks — or job sites, equipment, bookings, patients, commissions. Whatever your business runs on becomes a first-class part of the system, with the same tables, boards, search and export as anything built in.

Five minutes, start to finish

This is the whole setup — not a trimmed-down trial version of it.

Choose what you track

Start from ready-made modules — Contacts, Deals, Tasks, Leads, Companies, Notes — and tick the ones that match how you work. Set your business name and currency while you're there.

Bring the spreadsheet you already have

Your real CRM today is probably a spreadsheet, so importing one is treated as the main event rather than an afterthought. Upload a CSV and columns are matched to fields automatically; anything unmatched can become a new field without leaving the screen.

It handles what spreadsheets actually produce: quoted commas, Excel's encoding, $1,234.00 and €1 234 as amounts, bracketed negatives, yes/no as tick boxes, and dates in whatever format the file happens to use.

Work your pipeline on a board

Any module with a dropdown gets a drag-and-drop board. Its columns are your own options, in your own order — Quoted, Scheduled, Invoiced, Paid, if that's the business you're in. Each column shows its count and total, so the board doubles as a live value report.

Build anything that's missing

Need something the templates don't cover? Name it, choose fields from eleven types — including links between modules — and it's ready. Under two minutes, no admin console, no support ticket.

Take it with you

Install it to your home screen or desktop. It keeps working with no connection at all — on site, in a van, in a basement — and syncs when you're back. Records added offline are never lost.

Questions worth asking

Can our whole team use it?

Today each person has their own account and their own workspace, syncing across their devices. Several people editing one shared workspace at the same time isn't what it does yet — we'd rather say so now than have you discover it later. Shared team workspaces are the next major piece of work.

Where does our data live?

On your device first, always. If you sign in, a copy syncs to the database behind your deployment. For businesses that need physical separation, we offer a dedicated deployment with its own database and its own domain.

What if we want to leave?

Export everything as JSON or per-module CSV, whenever you like, with no limits. It's also a standard web application, so you can host it yourself and keep running it without us.

How much data can it hold?

Comfortably into the thousands of records per workspace. Beyond that we'd move you onto dedicated infrastructure — which is a change we make, not a migration you have to run.

Does it connect to our other tools?

CSV in and out is the integration route today. There's no email sending or third-party sync built in yet, and we'd rather be straight about that than imply otherwise.
